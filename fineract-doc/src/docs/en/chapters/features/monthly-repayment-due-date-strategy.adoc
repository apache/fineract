
= Monthly Repayment Due Date Strategy
:toc:
:toclevels: 2

https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/FINERACT-2816[FINERACT-2816]

== Goal

Apache Fineract currently adjusts monthly repayment due dates to the last day of the month when the original repayment day does not exist.

For example, a loan disbursed on *30 January* with monthly repayments has its first repayment due on *28 February*.

The goal is to introduce a configurable loan product strategy that allows the repayment due date to roll forward to the *first day of the following month* (1 March in this example), while preserving the original repayment day for subsequent installments.

The functionality must support both *cumulative* and *progressive* loan schedules and respect the configured interest calculation convention:

* *360/30:* Interest per regular monthly period remains unchanged under the specified monthly convention.
* *Actual/Actual:* Interest reflects the actual number of calendar days between repayment dates.

== Requirements

=== Functional Requirements

==== Repayment Due Date Configuration

Introduce a new loan product attribute: `monthEndDueDateStrategy`.

Supported values:

* `LAST_DAY_OF_MONTH` (default): Use the last day of the target month when the original repayment day does not exist.
* `FIRST_DAY_OF_NEXT_MONTH`: Roll the repayment due date to the first day of the following month.

The functionality must:

* Apply to monthly repayment frequencies only.
* Support cumulative and progressive loan schedules.
* Preserve the original repayment day for subsequent installments.
* Apply to all repayment periods, including the final installment.
* Respect existing meeting calendar, holiday, and working-day adjustment rules.

==== Repayment Date Example

Loan disbursed on 30 January 2025 with monthly repayments:

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Period |LAST_DAY_OF_MONTH |FIRST_DAY_OF_NEXT_MONTH

|1 |28 February |1 March
|2 |30 March |30 March
|3 |30 April |30 April
|===

==== Interest Calculation — 360/30

Given a loan product configured with 360 days per year and 30 days per month, rolling a repayment date must not change the interest calculated for each regular monthly period under the specified 360/30 convention.

[cols="1,2,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|Period |LAST_DAY_OF_MONTH |FIRST_DAY_OF_NEXT_MONTH |Interest fraction

|1 |30 Jan - 28 Feb |30 Jan - 1 Mar |30/360
|2 |28 Feb - 30 Mar |1 Mar - 30 Mar |30/360
|===

*Expected result:* Interest per period remains unchanged, assuming equivalent outstanding principal balances.

==== Interest Calculation — Actual/Actual

Given a loan product configured with Actual/Actual, interest must be calculated using the actual number of calendar days between repayment dates.

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Period |LAST_DAY_OF_MONTH |FIRST_DAY_OF_NEXT_MONTH

|1 |29/365 (30 Jan - 28 Feb) |30/365 (30 Jan - 1 Mar)
|2 |30/365 (28 Feb - 30 Mar) |29/365 (1 Mar - 30 Mar)
|===

*Expected result:*

* The first period accrues one additional day of interest on the higher outstanding principal balance.
* The second period accrues one fewer day of interest on the reduced outstanding principal balance.
* Total interest may increase because interest is calculated on different outstanding principal balances.

==== API and Persistence

* Expose `monthEndDueDateStrategy` in loan product create, update, read, and template APIs.
* Store the configured strategy on both the loan product and loan account.
* Loan accounts must inherit the strategy from the loan product at submission.
* Changing the loan product configuration must not affect existing loans.
* Schedule regeneration and interest recalculation must reuse the strategy stored on the loan account.

=== Non-Functional Requirements

* *Backward compatibility:* Existing loan products and loans must retain their current repayment schedules and interest calculations.
* *Financial accuracy:* The new strategy must respect existing day-count conventions, rounding rules, and principal-interest allocation.
* *Consistency:* Initial schedule generation, rescheduling, fixed-length maturity calculations, and holiday processing must apply the strategy consistently.
* *Test coverage:* Automated tests must cover both schedule types, leap years, month-end dates, 360/30, Actual/Actual, and schedule regeneration.
* *Performance:* The new configuration must not introduce significant overhead during repayment schedule generation.

== Configuration

=== Loan Product Attribute

The repayment due date strategy is configured at the loan product level using the `monthEndDueDateStrategy` attribute.

=== Available Values

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Value |Description

|`LAST_DAY_OF_MONTH`
|Moves the repayment due date to the last day of the target month when the original repayment day does not exist. This is the default behavior.

|`FIRST_DAY_OF_NEXT_MONTH`
|Moves the repayment due date to the first day of the following month when the original repayment day does not exist.
|===

=== Example Loan Product Configuration

[source,json]
----
{
  "repaymentEvery": 1,
  "repaymentFrequencyType": 2,
  "monthEndDueDateStrategy": "FIRST_DAY_OF_NEXT_MONTH"
}
----

The example contains only the relevant configuration attributes, not a complete loan product creation request.

=== Configuration Rules

* The default strategy is `LAST_DAY_OF_MONTH`.
* The configuration applies only to monthly repayment frequencies.
* Both cumulative and progressive loan products are supported.
* The strategy applies to all repayment due dates, including the final installment.
* The original repayment anchor day is preserved.
* Loan accounts inherit the strategy from the loan product at submission.
* Changing the loan product configuration does not affect existing loans.
* Schedule regeneration uses the strategy stored on the loan account.
* Meeting calendars take precedence over the configured strategy.
* Existing working-day and holiday adjustments continue to apply.

=== Interaction with Interest Calculation

The `monthEndDueDateStrategy` attribute controls repayment due dates and does not modify the loan product's interest calculation configuration.

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Day-count convention |Expected behavior

|360/30
|Interest per regular monthly period remains unchanged under the specified 30/360 monthly convention.

|Actual/Actual
|Interest is calculated using the actual calendar days between consecutive repayment dates. Rolling a due date can change the interest amount.
|===

NOTE: The expected 360/30 behavior must be verified against Fineract's day-count implementation, especially for periods such as 1 March to 30 March.
