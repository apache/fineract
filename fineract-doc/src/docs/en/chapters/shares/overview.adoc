= Overview

== Concepts

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|*Concept* |*Description*

|*Share product*
|A template defining the equity instrument that clients subscribe to: total authorized shares, unit
price, market price history, minimum/nominal/maximum shares per client, lock-in period, dividend
eligibility settings, applicable charges and accounting rule. Backed by `ShareProduct`
(`m_share_product`).

|*Share account*
|A client's subscription to a share product. It tracks approved and pending shares, the linked
savings account used for dividend payouts and the account lifecycle status. Backed by `ShareAccount`
(`m_share_account`).

|*Share transaction*
|An individual purchase, redemption or charge-payment movement on a share account. Backed by
`ShareAccountTransaction` (`m_share_account_transactions`).

|*Share charge*
|A fee applied to a share account at share-purchase, activation or other charge times. Backed by
`ShareAccountCharge` (`m_share_account_charge`).

|*Dividend*
|A distribution declared at the product level for a period and split across the eligible active
accounts. Backed by `ShareProductDividendPayOutDetails` (`m_share_product_dividend_pay_out`) and
`ShareAccountDividendDetails` (`m_share_account_dividend_details`).
|===

== Module layout

[cols="2,3"]
|===
|*Layer* |*Location*

|Domain entities, enums, repositories
|`fineract-shares` — `org.apache.fineract.portfolio.shareaccounts.domain`,
`org.apache.fineract.portfolio.shareproducts.domain`

|REST resources
|`fineract-provider` — `AccountsApiResource`, `ProductsApiResource`, `ShareDividendApiResource`

|Command handlers
|`fineract-provider` — `shareaccounts.handler`, `shareproducts.handler`

|Write / read services
|`fineract-provider` — `shareaccounts.service`, `shareproducts.service`

|Validation / serialization
|`fineract-provider` — `ShareAccountDataSerializer`, `ShareProductDataSerializer`

|Scheduled dividend posting
|`fineract-provider` — `shareaccounts.jobs.postdividentsforshares`
|===

NOTE: The module is gated by `SharesModuleIsEnabledCondition`
(`fineract-shares/.../shareaccounts/config/SharesModuleIsEnabledCondition.java`). When the module is
disabled, the shares beans are not registered.
