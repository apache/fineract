= Business rules

[cols="1,1,3,1,2"]
|===
|*ID* |*Category* |*Rule* |*Type* |*Origin in code*

|RN-001 |Product |Product `name` ≤ 200 chars, `shortName` ≤ 4 chars, `description` ≤ 500 chars. |Validation |`ShareProductDataSerializer`
|RN-002 |Product |A share product defines `totalShares`, `unitPrice` and derived `shareCapital` (`totalShares` × `unitPrice`). |Invariant |`ShareProduct`
|RN-003 |Product |Per-account subscription must respect `minimumShares` ≤ requested ≤ `maximumShares`; `nominalShares` is the default. |Validation |`ShareProduct` / `ShareAccountDataSerializer`
|RN-004 |Product |Market price is effective from `fromDate`; redemption/dividend valuation uses the price applicable at the operation date. |Calculation |`ShareProductMarketPrice`
|RN-005 |Account |A new account starts in `SUBMITTED_AND_PENDING_APPROVAL`; its shares are pending until approved. |Lifecycle |`ShareAccountStatusType`, `ShareAccount`
|RN-006 |Account |An account must be `APPROVED` before it can be `ACTIVE`; only active accounts allow additional purchases and redemptions. |Precondition |`ShareAccountWritePlatformService`
|RN-007 |Account |`undoApproval` returns an `APPROVED` account to `SUBMITTED_AND_PENDING_APPROVAL`. |Lifecycle |`ShareAccountWritePlatformService.undoApproveShareAccount`
|RN-008 |Transaction |Applying for shares creates a `PURCHASED`/`APPLIED` transaction and increases `totalSharesPending`; approval moves it to `APPROVED` and increases `totalSharesApproved`. |State change |`ShareAccountTransaction`, `PurchasedSharesStatusType`
|RN-009 |Transaction |Total issued shares across accounts cannot exceed the product's authorized `totalShares`. |Constraint |`IssueableSharesExceededException`
|RN-010 |Transaction |Redemption is blocked while the share lot is within the account/product lock-in period. |Precondition |`ShareAccount` (`lockinPeriodFrequency`), `ShareProduct.lockinPeriod`
|RN-011 |Charge |A share charge is FLAT or PERCENT_OF_AMOUNT; percentage charges are computed on the transaction amount. |Calculation |`ShareAccountCharge` (`ChargeCalculationType`)
|RN-012 |Charge |Charges are tracked as paid / waived / written-off with a derived outstanding balance. |Invariant |`ShareAccountCharge`
|RN-013 |Dividend |A dividend is declared per product for a period and split across accounts active during that period. |Calculation |`ShareProductDividendPayOutDetails`, `ShareAccountDividendDetails`
|RN-014 |Dividend |An account must meet the product's `minimumActivePeriod` to be eligible, unless `allowDividendCalculationForInactiveClients` is set. |Eligibility |`ShareProduct`, `ShareAccount`
|RN-015 |Dividend |A dividend declaration must be `APPROVED` before the scheduled job can post it. |Precondition |`ShareProductDividendStatusType`
|RN-016 |Dividend |Posting deposits the dividend into the account's linked savings account and records `savingsTransactionId`; per-account status becomes `POSTED`. |State change |`PostDividentsForSharesTasklet`, `ShareAccountDividendStatusType`
|===

IMPORTANT: This catalog summarizes the rules enforced in the shares domain and its serializers. The
referenced classes are the authoritative source; consult them for exact thresholds and error codes.
