= Shares

The *Shares* module lets a financial institution issue equity-like _share products_ and open
_share accounts_ for clients who subscribe to those shares. It models the full lifecycle of a
subscription — application, approval, activation, purchase of additional shares, redemption and
closure — together with the charges levied on each operation and the periodic distribution of
dividends to subscribers.

The domain layer lives in the `fineract-shares` Gradle module
(`org.apache.fineract.portfolio.shareaccounts` and `org.apache.fineract.portfolio.shareproducts`),
while the orchestration (command handlers, REST resources, write services and serializers) currently
remains in `fineract-provider`. The module is conditionally activated through
`SharesModuleIsEnabledCondition`.

This chapter documents the entities, the transaction and status model, the business rules, the
process flows and the REST contract of the module.
