= Office hierarchy access

Fineract represents an office hierarchy as a dot-delimited path. The head
office uses `"."`, while child offices append their identifiers, for example
`.1.`, `.1.2.`, and `.1.2.3.`. Access checks use this path as a prefix:
users can access resources in their own office and subordinate offices, but
not resources belonging to a parent or unrelated office.

Account transfers validate the office hierarchy of both the source and target
accounts before processing. This applies to savings-to-savings,
savings-to-loan, and loan-to-savings transfers.

Loan and savings write operations also load accounts in the authenticated
user's office scope. Users can perform these operations only on accounts in
their own office or a subordinate office:

* loans: close, close as rescheduled, loan officer assignment (single and
  bulk) and removal, adding, removing and updating tranches, and marking as
  fraud
* savings accounts: interest calculation, charge inactivation, field officer
  assignment and removal, withholding tax updates, and blocking or unblocking
  the account, its credits or its debits

Operations run by scheduled jobs, such as escheat, dormancy handling and
interest recalculation, are not restricted this way.
